Channel Uploader

Channel Uploader is a private command-line tool used by a single independent creator to upload their own videos to the YouTube channels they own:

  • Quiz Your Genius
  • Nightsketchs

It uses the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload) to upload videos, set their title, description, tags, privacy status and publish time, and set thumbnails. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
